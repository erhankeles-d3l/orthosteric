## What this table establishes

1. **No per-isoform ATP Km value from Somoza et al. 2015 is established by this evidence review.** Only a combined range across three isoforms is confirmed.
2. **The "100 µM for PI3Kδ" figure is weaker evidence than previously characterized.** It is sponsor documentation citing an internal, unpublished/unlocated reference repeated across multiple clinical protocols — not independent primary kinetic corroboration.
3. **The Lcc<<N graph-connectivity conclusion is model-dependent**, not a robust property of the ADR-0003 problem. A plausible alternative graph-generation assumption (clustered/hub studies) produces a qualitatively different result (Lcc ≈ 97 vs ≈ 26, with much larger variance).
